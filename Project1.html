Project 1: The Empathy Map

UX/UI Design Internship

Understanding the needs of busy office professionals

👤 User Persona

👨‍💻

Rahul Sharma

Age: 28 Years

Occupation: Software Developer

Working Hours: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM

Location: Lucknow

Rahul is a busy software developer who spends most of his day working at a desk. He wants to stay fit but struggles to find enough time for exercise.

🎯 Goals

  • Stay physically active
  • Improve overall health
  • Reduce work stress
  • Exercise regularly
  • Find short workouts
  • Exercise without going to the gym

😫 Frustrations

  • Lack of time
  • Long workout sessions
  • Office fatigue
  • Back-to-back meetings
  • No time to visit the gym
  • Complicated fitness apps

❤️ Empathy Map

🗣️ SAYS

  • "I don't have enough time to exercise."
  • "I need short workouts."
  • "Going to the gym is difficult."
  • "I can't spend an hour working out."
  • "I want something I can do during my workday."

🧠 THINKS

  • "I should take better care of my health."
  • "Where will I find time to exercise?"
  • "Can a 5-10 minute workout help?"
  • "I need something simple."
  • "I am becoming less active."

👀 DOES

  • Works long hours at a desk
  • Attends back-to-back meetings
  • Sits for long periods
  • Postpones exercise
  • Exercises occasionally on weekends
  • Uses mobile fitness content

❤️ FEELS

  • Tired
  • Stressed
  • Guilty
  • Burned out
  • Physically stiff
  • Unmotivated

⚠️ User Pain Points

1. Lack of Time: Busy work schedule leaves little time for exercise.
2. Long Workouts: Traditional workouts take too much time.
3. Fatigue: The user feels tired after a long workday.
4. No Gym Access: Travelling to a gym is inconvenient.
5. Complex Apps: Complicated interfaces make fitness apps difficult to use.

💡 Key Insights

  • The user needs short workouts that fit into a busy workday.
  • The user prefers convenience over long gym sessions.
  • The fitness experience should be simple and quick.
  • Micro-workouts can help users exercise during the workday.
  • The app should reduce friction rather than add another task.
